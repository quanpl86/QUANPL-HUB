## 9. Root-cause assessment

### Confirmed
1. The site is crawlable and indexable in principle.
2. Sitemap is valid and downloadable by Google.
3. Some content pages are already indexed.
4. Many strategic AI/Education pages are either **unknown** or only **discovered**, with no crawl time.
5. Organic visibility is extremely low: **16 impressions / 1 click in 28 days**.
6. Search query coverage is almost nonexistent.
7. Baseline had a global `Organization.logo` defect; **production fix deployed 2026-09-27 13:10 GMT+7, awaiting Google re-crawl/report refresh**.

### Most likely primary bottlenecks
1. **Weak crawl/discovery pathways for newer strategic posts.**
2. **Low domain/page authority.**
3. **Topic clusters not yet strong enough to signal priority.**
4. **Newer URLs have not accumulated crawl history.**
5. **Lack of external citations/backlinks.**
6. **Possible over-reliance on sitemap instead of strong HTML internal-link discovery.**
